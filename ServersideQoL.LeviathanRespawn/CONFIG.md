<details open><summary><b>LeviathanRespawn</b></summary>

|Option|Default Value|Acceptable Values|Description|
|------|-------------|-----------------|-----------|
|Enabled|true|True/False|Enables/disables the entire mod|
|RespawnDelayMinMinutes|60|From 0 to 100000|Minimum delay (in-game minutes) before a sunk Leviathan respawns|
|RespawnDelayMaxMinutes|240|From 0 to 100000|Maximum delay (in-game minutes) before a sunk Leviathan respawns|
|RespawnOffsetMin|300||Minimum distance (meters) a respawned Leviathan is relocated from the original position|
|RespawnOffsetMax|1200||Maximum distance (meters) a respawned Leviathan is relocated from the original position|
|RespawnChance|100|From 0 to 100|The probability a sunk Leviathan will respawn|
|MaxCheckIntervalSeconds|60||Maximum interval (real-time seconds) between checks whether a pending respawn is due|
|IncludeLeviathanLava|false|True/False|Also respawn LeviathanLava (flametal) Leviathans|
